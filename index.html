The gossip was always about brands.

How GupShaap began.

“Did you see who they signed?” is a perfectly normal opening line for us. Twenty minutes later, we’re debating whether the new ambassador makes perfect sense for the brand, what the campaign did with that pairing, and who we’d have called instead.

A product launch gets a full debrief. Why that venue? Who made the guest list? Did the product get its moment? And which brand just sent out a PR hamper so outrageous we’re still talking about the box?

That’s how our conversations go. A campaign from two weeks ago comes up over coffee. Last month’s trend gets a post-mortem. One of us spots something new and starts making a case for why it’s about to be everywhere.

Somewhere in those conversations, we’d always end up sketching out an idea of our own. We loved the debrief. We wanted to write the brief, too.

So here we are with a little GupShaap of our own, ready to make your brand the talk of the town.

Shruti & Ishika
Co-founders, GupShaap
